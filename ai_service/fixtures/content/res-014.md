# Attention Is All You Need (summary excerpt)

## Approach
The Transformer replaces recurrence and convolution with stacked self-attention and position-wise feed-forward layers in an encoder-decoder architecture. Multi-head attention lets the model attend to information from different representation subspaces. Positional encodings inject token order.

## Findings
The model achieved state-of-the-art machine translation quality at the time while training significantly faster thanks to parallelisable attention computation.

## Significance
The architecture became the foundation of modern large language models, including the generators used in retrieval-augmented generation systems.
