# Dense Passage Retrieval for Open-Domain Question Answering (summary excerpt)

## Approach
A dual-encoder model embeds questions and passages separately with BERT-based encoders. Retrieval is maximum inner product search over precomputed passage embeddings. Training uses in-batch negatives plus hard negatives mined with BM25.

## Findings
Dense retrieval substantially outperformed a strong BM25 baseline on top-20 passage retrieval accuracy across several QA datasets, and improved end-to-end QA accuracy.

## Relevance
DPR is the retriever component later reused in the original RAG model.
