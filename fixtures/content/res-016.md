# Self-RAG: Learning to Retrieve, Generate, and Critique through Self-Reflection (summary excerpt)

## Approach
Instead of always retrieving a fixed number of passages, a single language model is trained to emit special reflection tokens. These decide whether retrieval is needed, judge whether retrieved passages are relevant, and assess whether its own output is supported by the evidence.

## Findings
Adaptive retrieval and self-critique improved factuality and citation accuracy compared with standard retrieval-augmented baselines on several tasks.

## Comparison point
Compared with the original RAG approach, Self-RAG retrieves on demand and critiques its outputs, while RAG retrieves for every input and does not self-assess.
