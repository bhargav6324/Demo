# Hybrid Retrieval Methods for University Library Discovery (embargoed dissertation excerpt)

RESTRICTED: available to faculty, researchers and librarians only until the embargo ends.

## Abstract
This dissertation evaluates combining BM25 sparse retrieval with dense embeddings and a cross-encoder re-ranker over a university catalog of 1.2 million records. Hybrid retrieval improved nDCG@10 by 14 percent over dense-only retrieval on a set of real student queries, with the largest gains for short, keyword-style searches.
